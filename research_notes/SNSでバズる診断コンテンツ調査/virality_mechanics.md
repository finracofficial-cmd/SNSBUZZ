# Virality Mechanics of UGC Diagnosis / Generator / Quiz Web Content among Japanese 10s–20s (TikTok, Instagram, X), 2025–Oct 2026

Scope note: research conducted 2026-10-23. Data is 2025–2026 unless flagged with a date. Most quantitative "case" numbers in Japan come from vendor press releases (Pickles/ヨミトル, ギミックプラス/Makko) or company self-reports and are not independently audited; this is flagged inline. Academic sources are older (2012 etc.) and flagged.

## Key Question 1: Psychology — why do Japanese 10s–20s share diagnosis/generator results?

### Takeaway
Sharing a result is cheap, face-free self-disclosure that doubles as a conversation starter: Gen Z in Japan treat type labels (MBTI 4 letters, ラブタイプ, テト/エゲン) as a shared vocabulary for 自己紹介, compatibility talk and celebrity/推し comparison, while classic drivers (intrinsic reward of self-disclosure, favorable Barnum-style descriptions, high-arousal emotion) explain why a result feels worth posting. A 2025–2026 counter-signal: plain MBTI is saturating ("物足りない"), pushing demand toward more granular, character-rich or emotionally intense formats.

### Cited Findings
**Universal mechanisms (academic; older, flagged)**
- Self-disclosure is intrinsically rewarding: talking about oneself activated the mesolimbic dopamine system (nucleus accumbens, VTA), and participants were willing to forgo money to disclose about themselves (Tamir & Mitchell, PNAS, May 2012; lab studies). Press coverage claimed subjects gave up ~a quarter of potential earnings — that figure is from press, not verified against the paper. — [PMC: Tamir & Mitchell 2012](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3361411/); [Scientific American podcast](https://www.scientificamerican.com/podcast/episode/sharing-opinions-feels-at-least-as-12-05-10)
- Berger & Milkman (Journal of Marketing Research, 2012) analyzed ~7,000 NYT articles: content evoking high-arousal emotions (awe; anger/anxiety) is more viral; low-arousal sadness is less viral; positive content is more viral than negative; surprising, interesting and practically useful content is also positively linked to sharing. — [Berger & Milkman PDF](https://cssh.northeastern.edu/pandemic-teaching-initiative/wp-content/uploads/sites/43/2020/09/What-Makes-Online-Content-Viral.pdf)
- Barnum/Forer effect: people rate vague, generic personality descriptions as highly accurate; favorable descriptions are accepted more readily than unfavorable ones (Pollyanna principle); acceptance depends on relevance and favorability of the profile (Dickson & Kelly 1985 review). — [Wikipedia: Barnum effect](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barnum_effect); [Review of Barnum effect literature (PDF)](https://cortecs.org/wp-content/uploads/2014/12/the-barnum-effect-in-personality-assessment-a-review-of-the-literature.pdf)
- A 2025 study found skepticism did not significantly reduce Barnum susceptibility; three traits together explained only 10.8% of variance (i.e., "ネタだとわかっていても当たってると感じる" is robust). — [IJIP 2025 (PDF)](https://ijip.in/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/18.01.337.20251303.pdf)
- Qualitative thesis on BuzzFeed quizzes (Roger Williams Univ., 2017; focus group + 5 interviews): sharing a quiz result signals a perceived identity/self-view — quizzes act as self-presentation tools. Small qualitative study. — [RWU thesis](https://docs.rwu.edu/cgi/viewcontent.cgi?article=1000&context=communication_theses)
- Vendor claim (unverified): in a B2C case study >3/4 of shared quiz results contained positive trigger words; results are "almost always positive" to encourage sharing. — [LeadQuizzes](https://www.leadquizzes.com/blog/how-to-make-buzzfeed-quiz/)
- Scale precedent (2007–2018, old): Cambridge's myPersonality Facebook app had >6 million test-takers, nearly half agreeing to share Facebook profile data with the project. — [Madhyamam / New Scientist report, May 2018](https://english.madhyamam.com/science-technology/2018/may/15/app-exposed-over-3-mn-facebook-users-data-years-report)

**Japanese Gen Z: type labels as shared vocabulary (2025–2026)**

Framing: "MBTIは答えより会話を始める装置". (Vendor consultancy; sampling not disclosed.) — [PR TIMES ch.8, 2026-09-08](https://prtimes.jp/main/html/rd/p/000000104.000104461.html); [PR TIMES ch.1](https://prtimes.jp/main/html/rd/p/000000097.000104461.html)
- Same survey: 56.2% have taken MBTI diagnosis multiple times; of those 281, 85.4% know their type's traits. — [PR TIMES ch.6](https://prtimes.jp/main/html/rd/p/000000102.000104461.html)
- Same survey: of 479 who view MBTI content, "あるある動画" is most viewed (68.3%); 相性診断 viewed by 43.4%, but respondents "don't treat compatibility as the answer to the relationship". — [PR TIMES ch.7](https://prtimes.jp/main/html/rd/p/000000103.000104461.html)
- Older (date unclear, ~2023) MERY survey: diagnosis experience 41.3% for Z世代 vs 17.4% for 30–40s; 60%+ of Z世代 takers know the MBTI types of people around them (i.e., results are socially circulated). — [FINDERS](https://finders.me/kqFQxAjVYZiWKLhlxQ); [Excite News](https://www.excite.co.jp/news/article/AMP_434254/)
- Toyo Keizai (2026-02-26): Gen Z uses MBTI in 自己紹介 and SNS profiles and checks compatibility when meeting people (e.g., "私は主人公（ENFJ）です"); valued for タイパ (conveys personality in one word); but MBTI alone is "物足りなくなってきている", driving "超細分化" diagnoses such as 64-type love compatibility. — [東洋経済オンライン](https://toyokeizai.net/articles/-/935044?display=b)
- Self-introduction use is seasonal: a note author reports MBTI types flying around during university 新学期/新歓 self-introductions. — [note: サルフラワー](https://note.com/sulflower0255/n/n2d91cc0b0709)

**Next-wave "type tribes" (Korea → Japan)**
- テト/エゲン (testosterone/estrogen) 4-type classification: originated from a Korean Instagram manga artist (spring 2024), spread via TikTok/Instagram diagnosis tests in 2025; heavily used for romance compatibility ("エゲン男がテト女に惹かれる") and labeling K-pop idols/celebrities (BTS Jungkook = テト男, etc.). — [Yahoo!ニュース エキスパート (西嶋広美)](https://news.yahoo.co.jp/expert/articles/ff566917eb602f48f2277a566a4b44c8011325dc)
- Forbes JAPAN (2025-08-06): a 28-question テト-エゲン test had 630,000+ participants; trend arose after MBTI "飽きられた" in Korea; used as icebreaker, ideal-partner search and celebrity labeling. — [Forbes JAPAN](https://forbesjapan.com/articles/detail/81078)
- 現代ビジネス frames the trend as Korean youth "typing themselves" more than blood-type fortune, to avoid failure in romance/marriage. — [gendai.media](https://gendai.media/articles/-/170757)

**Identity, 推し comparison, emotion**
- Fruitful Personas (designer case study, June 2025): ~300,000+ participants (self-estimated from tags/DMs/views); K-pop fans posted their fruit persona next to their favorite idol's; results described platonic/romantic compatibility; creator notes "accuracy isn't required for resonance". — [Contra case study](https://contra.com/p/Akv6mO22-fruitful-personas-a-viral-personality-quiz)
- 『80億分の1』(Oct 2026, life simulator built on UN/WHO/World Bank stats): Japanese X reactions were high-arousal and comparative — "死んだときに泣いてしまった", "幸せってなんだろうと考えさせられる", many saying they felt lucky to be born in Japan. — [ASCII.jp 2026-10-15](https://ascii.jp/elem/000/004/442/4442836/); [coki 2026-10-14](https://coki.jp/article/column/95340/)
- SHIBUYA109 lab. (July 2025, n=413 women 15–24, Tokyo area): 75.6% are reluctant to post photos showing their face/style; only 28.3% often use the word "映え"; lab director: shift from uniform 映え to expressing one's own 世界観; companies should design experiences young people want to "切り取りたい". — [SHIBUYA109 lab. 2025-08-26](https://www.shibuya109.co.jp/shibuya109lab/reports/250826/); [AdverTimes 2025-08-28](https://www.advertimes.com/20250828/article509730/)
- 界隈 culture is the dominant SNS meme grammar in 2025: SHIBUYA109 lab. トレンド大賞2025 (n=619 women 15–24, Sep–Oct 2025) SNS meme winners include 今これ界隈, "〇〇すぎてしぬ‼️" (しぬしぬ界隈), plus もんた界隈 / 国宝界隈 imitation memes; experience category: ぬい活, シール帳 (collect & trade with friends). No diagnosis item made the list. — [SHIBUYA109 lab. トレンド大賞2025](https://www.shibuya109.co.jp/shibuya109lab/reports/251111/)

### Inferences
- For Japanese 10s–20s, the core share motive is less "look how great I am" and more "this is my 界隈/type — talk to me about it": a result card is a low-risk, face-free self-disclosure (fits the 75.6% face-posting reluctance) and a conversation ticket (52.6% conversation-entry view; 自己紹介 use).
- "Rarity" framing (e.g., "80億分の1", "全体の3%") likely works as a uniqueness signal layered on top of belonging to a type; 80億分の1 used the number as the premise rather than a percentile, and still delivered high-arousal emotion — the emotion, not the number, drove posts.
- Barnum-style favorable copy + one "痛いところを突く" line is consistent with the literature: favorability drives acceptance, while a mildly negative/self-deprecating line gives ネタ value and "わかる" reactions (mapped to the あるある video popularity, 68.3%). The negative line should be framed as lovable, not insulting (unfavorable claims are accepted less).
- MBTI saturation implies new launches should either (a) piggyback on known 4-letter/16-type grammar with a novel twist (e.g., 64 types, love, 推し, animals/food), or (b) introduce a new binary/quadrant label that is easy to apply to celebrities (テト/エゲン model).

### Gaps
- No Japan-specific survey found that directly measures "% of 10s–20s who have posted a diagnosis result" or which motive (identity vs humor vs compatibility) dominates.
- No rigorous study found on rarity/percentile framing ("あなたは上位3%") and share rate.
- Academic work on 推し活 or 界隈 as sharing drivers for quizzes specifically was not found; links above are trend reports, not causal studies.

## Key Question 2: Share-asset design — what result-page/image elements drive sharing (with numbers)?

### Takeaway
Hard A/B data is almost nonexistent publicly; the best available numbers are vendor/company case figures showing share-to-play ratios of ~18% for typical brand diagnoses and up to 85%+ for a well-designed one, completion of ~70–90% for short quizzes, and a consistent design pattern: a named type/character image + "〇〇タイプ" share text, no sign-up, few questions (3–7 for reach), a downloadable/savable asset (icon, certificate, card), and IP/character tie-ins.

### Cited Findings
**Quantified Japanese cases (company/vendor-reported)**
- Mercari "人生のくすぶり度診断": 15,000+ completions in first week, share rate >85% (Pickles/ヨミトル press release, 2026-09-02; vendor-reported). — [PR TIMES (Pickles)](https://prtimes.jp/main/html/rd/p/000000205.000002036.html)
- Pickles analysis of 156 diagnosis cases (H1 2026): short formats (3–5 questions) used for reach/awareness, long (up to 44 questions) for lead quality; "ちょい見せ" teaser (show part of result, withhold rest) is key for LINE funnels; ~17% of completers registered on LINE, ~20% is a "solid target"; BtoC uses: SNS buzz/campaigns 32%, product understanding/purchase 28%. — [PR TIMES (Pickles), 2026-09-02](https://prtimes.jp/main/html/rd/p/000000205.000002036.html)
- タカラトミー "デュエマ文明診断": 110,000+ plays on launch day, 20,000+ shares (≈18% share/play); 4 everyday questions so non-fans can play; results grouped into 16 types. — [ヨミトル トレンド (shindancloud)](https://shindancloud.com/trend/624)
- ピクルス "16タイプ マーケター診断2" (Oct 2022, older): 55,000 plays, 10,000+ shares in one month (≈18%). — [shindancloud](https://shindancloud.com/trend/624)
- ディップ "バイトル×ぼっち・ざ・ろっく！おすすめバイト診断": 90,000 plays in first week, 90% completion rate; anime character tie-in drove organic sharing among young users. — [shindancloud](https://shindancloud.com/trend/624)
- メルカリ "あなたの「心の隠れキャラ」がバレる診断": downloadable result icons prompted repeat plays (no numbers). 大塚製薬 "ポカリIQチェック": six knowledge levels encouraged retries; 50,000 new followers in a week (campaign-linked). ### Inferences
- Benchmark heuristic from the cases: a "normal" brand diagnosis gets ~15–20 shares per 100 plays; a strong one (Mercari) can exceed 85%. Treat <10% as a design problem (result not identity-worthy or share flow too heavy).
- Viral coefficient (K) = (shares per user) × (clicks per share) × (click→completion). Using the cases: 0.18 shares/play × unknown CTR × ~0.7–0.9 completion. For K≥1 without paid push, share rate must be high AND each share must reach friends who click — implying in-image hooks ("あなたは何タイプ？", friend-compatibility prompts) matter as much as the share button.
- Recommended asset stack (inference, consistent with cases): (1) a 9:16 story card for IG Stories/TikTok (face-free, character art, type name ≤10 chars, one 痛いところ line, rarity %), (2) a 1.91:1 OGP/X card with the type name in large text, (3) a savable icon/sticker (Mercari icon, IQ certificate, Picrew-style avatar) that gets reused in profiles, (4) pre-written share text with "〇〇タイプ" + hashtag + URL.
- Keep 3–7 questions for viral/reach goals (Pickles 3–5, デュエマ 4 questions, completion 90% at ディップ); longer only if the result is the payoff (16Personalities-style).
- AI-personalized generators (analyzing a user's X posts, generating images) produce stronger "自分ごと" assets but carry per-user cost (¥10k/day at 3,000 users) and "AI-made" backlash risk (アマトリカ).

### Gaps
- No public A/B test on 9:16 vs square vs 1.91:1 share cards, rarity-% display, or presence of a negative line.
- No Japanese K-factor or click-per-share data found for any diagnosis.
- Share-rate definitions in vendor releases (e.g., Mercari >85%) are not defined (share button taps vs completed posts).

## Key Question 3: Platform behavior — how does diagnosis content spread on TikTok, Instagram, X, and what changed in 2025–2026?

### Takeaway
Instagram is the universal channel (≈90% use) but sharing happens in closed, ephemeral Stories among offline friends; X is the open "result-image + quote-repost" engine for hobby/fandom spread (and where Japanese buzz like 80億分の1 ignites); TikTok is the discovery engine (influencer-centric, trend source for 15–19 women), where carousels/photo posts now out-engage video. 2025–2026 platform changes favor DM "sends" on Instagram (plus native Reposts), and X has moved to in-app link opening and an open-sourced Grok-based ranker while creators still report weaker reach for external links.

### Cited Findings
**Usage and roles (Japan)**
- SHIBUYA109 lab. (July 2025, n=413 women 15–24): use rates Instagram 89.6%, X 69.0%, TikTok 60.8%; main posting platform Instagram 45.5%, X 26.4%, BeReal 22.1%; on Instagram, Stories account for 38.1% of posting vs 7.4% feed/Reels; Stories content = memories with friends 67.4%; follow graphs: Instagram/BeReal = offline friends, TikTok = influencers, X = companies & hobby friends; discovery sources: Instagram 64.6%, TikTok 39.0%, X 35.1%, search engines 28.8%. — [SHIBUYA109 lab.](https://www.shibuya109.co.jp/shibuya109lab/reports/250826/)
- AdverTimes summary: SNS use is going "クローズド＆エフェメラル"; safe-to-post environment is now a prerequisite; some keep locked X accounts with ~5 close friends. — [AdverTimes 2025-08-28](https://www.advertimes.com/20250828/article509730/)

## Key Question 4: Seeding / launch tactics — owned account vs influencers, celebrity usage, timing, multi-language

### Takeaway
2025–2026 hits show a pattern: a solo/small builder ships fast, a "spark" post (developer's own Threads/X post, an influencer's reaction — even critical — or an official IP/brand account) ignites, then celebrities/public figures and media pickups accelerate; cross-border (Korea→Japan) diffusion with localized pages is now common. Timing hooks are 新学期/新歓 (自己紹介), late Nov–Dec year-in-review season, and trend-riding on fresh AI image tools.

## Key Question 5: Why most viral diagnosis sites decay quickly, and what sustains them

### Takeaway
Single-result diagnoses saturate once a user's friend graph has seen the type (one-and-done content), launch spikes rarely persist without a retention mechanism, and monetization is hard; long-lived examples sustain via UGC platforms (many creators producing new diagnoses), series/versioning (16→64 types), annual rituals (Wrapped), repeatable/variable outcomes (random lives, levels, gacha), and community features.

### Cited Findings
- れとるときゃりー: launch spikes don't persist because "盛り上がりを維持する仕組みがまだ十分ではなく"; "プロダクトにずっと生きていてほしいならば、やはりバズるだけではダメ"; revenue mechanism matters more than buzz; long-term aim is UGC/community-driven growth (like Zenn); ピクページ revenue nearly zero. — [レバテックLAB](https://levtech.jp/media/article/focus/detail_609/)
- Indie IQ test: after the 48h spike (3.2x PV), baseline settled at ~1.4x the pre-spike level. — [Qiita](https://qiita.com/tsukutte_mita920/items/77f41229f03ba8543ba2)
- Old precedent (2007): 脳内メーカー reached 500M cumulative PV between June 16 and Oct 5, 2007 — a name-input generator with explosive but era-bound virality. — [ITmedia オルタナティブ・ブログ (2007)](https://blogs.itmedia.co.jp/business20/2007/11/web_aad4.html)
- UGC platforms sustain: Makko診断ポータル aggregates diagnoses created by users of the Makko tool — 30M monthly PV after 8 months (2025-08-21). — [PR TIMES (ギミックプラス)](https://prtimes.jp/main/html/rd/p/000000004.000143217.html)
- Picrew (UGC avatar maker): recruiting page lists ~130M monthly PV, 74M+ users, 1.9M+ registered creators (undated company figures). — [talentsquare](https://talentsquare.co.jp/job/239-15762/)
- Series/versioning: LoveType16 evolved into 64-type ラブキャラ診断, inheriting the 16 characters. — [non-no web](https://nonno.hpplus.jp/lifestyle/topic/312485/); [iStep news](https://istep.click/news_blog/5297)
- Fatigue cycle: Korea's テト/エゲン trend arose after MBTI was "飽きられた" (Forbes JAPAN, 2025-08); Japanese Gen Z find MBTI alone "物足りない" (東洋経済 2026-02). — [Forbes JAPAN](https://forbesjapan.com/articles/detail/81078); [東洋経済](https://toyokeizai.net/articles/-/935044?display=b)
- Yet repeat use exists: 56.2% of Gen Z have taken MBTI multiple times (July 2025). — [PR TIMES ch.6](https://prtimes.jp/main/html/rd/p/000000102.000104461.html)
- Annual ritual: Spotify Wrapped grows yearly (2025: +19% YoY engaged users, shares +41% YoY to 500M+). — [Music Business Worldwide](https://www.musicbusinessworldwide.com/spotify-wrapped-campaign-hit-200m-engaged-users-in-24-hours-a-19-yoy-increase); [IBTimes](https://www.ibtimes.com/record-breaking-spotify-wrapped-2025-sees-200m-engage-500m-shares-day-3792059)
- Variable outcomes & community: 80億分の1 gives a random life each play (30–90 minutes each), includes a memorial feature (save a life, light a candle, leave a message), and is "ひとりでつくり、毎日直している" (daily fixes). — [coki](https://coki.jp/article/column/95340/)
- Repeat-play hooks in brand cases: ポカリIQチェック six levels encourage retries; メルカリ downloadable result icons prompt repeat plays. — [shindancloud](https://shindancloud.com/trend/624)
- Cost risk can kill longevity: AI-analysis diagnosis cost ~¥10,000/day for ~3,000 users. — [X post @lnkiai](https://x.com/lnkiai/status/2112129607612981510)
- Backlash risk: アマトリカ診断 faced "AI-made" criticism and complaints about repetitive characters after spreading. — [note: にー観測所](https://note.com/ni_intp/n/n6ee57a417690)
- Stereotype risk flagged for テト/エゲン (reinforcing 男性性/女性性 stereotypes); Korean users reportedly tiring of diagnoses. — [Yahoo!ニュース エキスパート](https://news.yahoo.co.jp/expert/articles/ff566917eb602f48f2277a566a4b44c8011325dc)

### Inferences
- Decay mechanics: (a) each user needs only one result; (b) the share loop exhausts when a friend cluster has all played; (c) novelty of the type taxonomy wears off; (d) costs (AI, servers) rise exactly when traffic peaks but monetization lags.
- Sustain levers, ranked by evidence strength: UGC creation platform (Makko, Picrew, 診断メーカー-style) > recurring ritual (annual/seasonal recap) > series/versioning of the same character universe (16→64; seasonal skins/limited types) > variable/random outcomes and levels that justify replays (80億分の1, ポカリ) > friend-to-friend loops (compatibility links requiring the other person to play) > community/memorial features.
- Plan the character IP as the durable asset (stickers, icons, merch-able art), with the diagnosis as the acquisition funnel; keep per-play marginal cost near zero (precompute results; generate images client-side or cache) to survive spikes.

- No data on how compatibility/friend-invite loops affect retention vs single-player diagnoses.
- 診断メーカー (shindanmaker.com) traffic/usage statistics could not be found in this research.
