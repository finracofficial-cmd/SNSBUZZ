### Inferences
- へびべあ fits on: a June 2026 peak, Vercel hosting, an MBTI base with original characters, a romance/compatibility angle, and a young audience. It conflicts on: the listing's "運営開始 2026年02月" and May i-mobile revenue (へびべあ launched May 29), X/VTuber spread versus the listing's short-video SNS, a vercel.app subdomain versus an お名前.com custom domain, and a "creative unit" versus a corporate seller. Verdict: not confirmed. A buyer should ask for the URL in negotiation.
- Derived unit economics (my calculation from listing figures): June revenue per 1,000 PV was about ¥102 (¥1.99M / 19.45M PV). September was about ¥668 (¥584K / 874K PV), a roughly 6.5x increase. Either viral-spike traffic monetizes very poorly (consistent with the Qiita case in Q5), or Ad Manager MCM or a different PV basis inflated later RPM. This anomaly should be verified in due diligence.
- PV fell about 95.5% from June to September, and about 98% by October's estimate. If the October estimate of about 400K PV holds at September's RPM, October revenue would be about ¥270K, and the asking price would be about 47x the forward monthly profit, not 23x.
- Infra at peak was about 3.5% of revenue. Technically the format can scale to tens of millions of PV at near-zero marginal cost. The binding constraint is distribution (SNS operation), not technology.
- The average monthly PV of 4.01M implies roughly 32M PV over Feb–Sep, if averaged over 8 months. Against 4M completions, that is about 8 PV per completion.

### Gaps
- Site identity: no public article, X post, or press release matches "Feb 2026 launch + Korean version + LINE stamps + bulletin board + 4M completions." The seller deliberately withholds the URL.
- July–August PV figures are not broken out in the listing.
- No information on SNS account names or follower counts, or on LINE stamp sales.

## Q1. How big is the MBTI / 16Personalities boom in Japan and Korea, and is it growing, plateauing, or fatiguing?

### Takeaway
In Japan, MBTI is now near-universal among 18–24s: 97.6% know it and 96.4% have taken it (2025 survey). It has become a "common language" rather than a novelty. Commentators describe a shift toward derivative, more fine-grained diagnoses (romance, 64 types) rather than outright fatigue. Korea, the origin of the boom, shows clearer signs of cooling and rotation to new tests (에겐/테토, SBTI, HSP, attachment style).

### Cited Findings
- Z-SOZOKEN (Fiom), online survey Jun–Jul 2025, n=500, ages 18–24 nationwide. 97.6% know MBTI (0.6% only the name, 1.8% don't know it). 96.4% have taken a diagnosis; 56.2% have taken it multiple times. 82.2% say it helped self-understanding. 48.4% find it easier to talk about themselves. 78.4% encounter MBTI posts on SNS (60.2% via feed, 18.2% via search). — [PR TIMES (Z-SOZOKEN)](https://prtimes.jp/main/html/rd/p/000000096.000104461.html)
- Same survey: 52.2% are interested in products or services linked to their type. That rises to 82.4% among people who actively search for MBTI content, versus 22.2% among those who rarely view it. MBTI content viewed: あるある videos 68.3%, compatibility diagnoses 43.4%, relationship analysis 36.3%. — [PR TIMES (Z-SOZOKEN)](https://prtimes.jp/main/html/rd/p/000000096.000104461.html)
- Z-SOZOKEN chapter 7 headline: Gen Z views compatibility diagnoses (43.4%) but "相性は関係の答えにしない" (doesn't treat compatibility as the answer to a relationship). — [PR TIMES ch.7](https://prtimes.jp/main/html/rd/p/000000103.000104461.html)
- MERY Z世代研究所, Sep 12–25, 2024, women only (older data). Gen Z women aged 18–29 (n=300): 67.3% aware of MBTI and 41.3% have taken it. Women in their 30s–40s (n=155): 24.5% and 17.4%. Among Gen Z takers, 62.9% know the types of people around them. Top perceived values: self-analysis 48.0%, conversation starter 40.1%. — [PR TIMES (MERY)](https://prtimes.jp/main/html/rd/p/000000118.000029212.html)
- Note the conflict: the 2024 MERY figure (67.3% aware, women 18–29) and the 2025 Z-SOZOKEN figure (97.6%, ages 18–24) differ a lot. Definitions and samples differ, so they should not be read as a trend line. — [MERY](https://prtimes.jp/main/html/rd/p/000000118.000029212.html); [Z-SOZOKEN](https://prtimes.jp/main/html/rd/p/000000096.000104461.html)
- Toyo Keizai, Feb 26, 2026, by 原田曜平: MBTI spread among Korean youth first, amplified by K-POP stars posting their types, then took root among Japanese Gen Z within a few years. Gen Z has begun finding MBTI alone "insufficient" and is moving to "超細分化" diagnoses, such as 64 types and romance-compatibility types. Blood-type diagnosis is fading with Gen Z and Gen Alpha. — [東洋経済オンライン](https://toyokeizai.net/articles/-/935044?display=b)
- AdverTimes (若者研究所), Oct 31, 2025: "MBTIの次は恋愛診断がZ世代のブームに". The article poses as an open question whether youth have tired of MBTI and are moving on to LoveType16. — [AdverTimes p.2](https://www.advertimes.com/20251031/article519500/2/)
- One 16-type site's aggregate of about 800K results: ESFP was most common (9.6%), ENTP least (as of Feb 2026, single-site data). — [motivation-up.com](https://motivation-up.com/assessment/16type/16type_rate.php)
- Korea, May 2025: "요즘은 MBTI 안 해요". The 에겐·테토 test was trending among people in their 20s–30s "in place of MBTI". — [Daum/News (2025-05-23)](https://v.daum.net/v/20250523163004140)
- Korea, Apr 2026: "MBTI는 끝났다? 요즘 뜨는 'SBTI'". SBTI describes oneself by consumption and behavior rather than personality. The article notes HSP and attachment-style tests also spreading. It has no participant numbers. — [CIVICNEWS](https://www.civicnews.com/news/articleView.html?idxno=39708)
- Korea, Jun 14, 2026 column (김경일): interest in MBTI has cooled. Criticism of using it in hiring and screening grew, and corporate and YouTube MBTI content faded. The author argues for a multidimensional (Big Five) view. The page text was partly garbled, so treat the details as approximate. — [Nate News column](https://m.news.nate.com/view/20260614n11272)
- Korea still shows MBTI-variant demand: 푸망 pushed a "2026년 NEW MBTI" retest in Jan 2026. — [Poomang on X](https://x.com/poomangofficial/status/2013474423614120052); [Poomang Threads](https://www.threads.com/@poomang_official/post/DTuD4N8koyu/)

### Inferences
- Japan is at a "saturation of awareness, not of engagement" stage. Almost every young person already has a type, so a new "find your MBTI" site has little novelty. Formats that use the known 4-letter type as an input or a layer (romance version, character skin, compatibility, 64-type refinement, AI report) are what are growing.
- Korea runs about 1–3 years ahead: MBTI peaked about 2021–2023, and rotation to 에겐/테토 (2025), SBTI, HSP, and attachment (2026) followed. That pattern suggests Japan's next demand is for new axes (gender-coded vibe, sensitivity, attachment, consumption), not more 16-type clones.

### Gaps
- No 2025–2026 Japanese teen (under 18) survey on MBTI penetration was found. Searches of マイナビティーンズラボ, SHIBUYA109 lab., and 博報堂 found no MBTI-specific release.
- No 2025–2026 대학내일20대연구소 MBTI figures were found.
- Google Trends / Naver DataLab search-volume curves were not retrieved, so the "plateau vs decline" call rests on commentary, not volume data.

## Q2. Which derivative/character-based diagnosis services hit big in Japan in 2024–2026?

### Takeaway
The clear category leader is 株式会社ラブキャラ's ラブタイプ診断 (LoveType16). It reports 100M cumulative diagnoses, 50M UU, 500K LINE friends, plus Taiwan and Korea versions, investment from 代々木アニメーショングループ, and expansion into an AI product. It shows the playbook of romance angle + character IP + MBTI-like 4-letter code + compatibility. 2026 brought a wave of fast-follower 16-type character diagnoses (へびべあ bears, 自己肯定感診断, 推し活/オタク診断, AI pair reports), plus "how others see you" and face-based types.

### Inferences
- The winning formula in Japan 2025–2026: an MBTI-like 4-letter code (instantly legible), cute animal or persona characters with "あるある" names, a romance or relationship framing, a share-ready image, and compatibility to pull in a second person. The listed site follows exactly this template, which also makes it easy to copy. Several near-identical 16-type character diagnoses appeared in May–June 2026 alone.
- Only ラブキャラ shows a path from "viral diagnosis" to "company" (LINE CRM of 500K, overseas versions, a media collab, investor capital, an AI product). Most others are one-shot sites by small units with no confirmed monetization beyond ads.
- Official big-IP (Sanrio, ちいかわ, anime) diagnoses are run as promotional campaigns by rights holders. An independent operator cannot easily enter there; original characters are the realistic route.

### Gaps
- Scale metrics (users, PV) for へびべあ, 自己肯定感診断, キャラクターコード, and the 64-type diagnoses were not found.
- No revenue figures for ラブキャラ were found. Its numbers are self-reported and undated.
- No verified 2025–2026 data on 動物キャラ-type (e.g., 動物占い revival) or 仕事タイプ diagnoses as consumer viral hits.

## Q3. Korea as a leading indicator: what worked, what crossed to Japan, and how operators monetized and sustained it

### Takeaway
Korean test platforms (케이테스트, 푸망) sustained traffic through high-volume, continuous production: about 100 tests in 2 years at K-Test, and 360+ tests at 푸망. Monetization came from ads plus advertiser/brand collaborations, affiliate (Coupang Partners), and courses. Big hits reached 10M+ visitors. Romance-tinted MBTI and LoveType16-style formats crossed into Japan and Taiwan. 에겐/테토 was Korea's 2025 post-MBTI hit, but I found no evidence it crossed to Japan at scale.

### Cited Findings
- 케이테스트 (CEO 김정빈), interview published May 27, 2023 (older data): about 100 tests built over about 2 years, at about 2 weeks per test. Average 5–10M participants per month. Three tests exceeded 10M visitors each: 퍼스널 컬러 테스트 (#1 on Naver real-time search for 3 days), 스마일 연애 테스트, and 라벨 스티커 테스트 (spread via SNS and community boards). Revenue came from advertiser inquiries that followed the viral hits. — [Harper's BAZAAR Korea](https://www.harpersbazaar.co.kr/article/77768)
- 케이테스트 has been developed and operated since 2020 by developer "코배투", who sells an Inflearn course on monetizing MBTI-test websites. The course covers AdSense and Coupang Partners affiliate insertion and optimization. — [Inflearn](https://www.inflearn.com/course/머니업라이브-mbti-웹사이트); [Inflearn JP](https://www.inflearn.com/ja/course/mbti테스트-수익형-웹사이트-코배투). Note the conflict: the founder's name and start year differ between the BAZAAR interview (~2021) and Inflearn (2020).
- 케이테스트's X account describes it as the "국내외 최대 MBTI 테스트 플랫폼" (self-description) and posts MBTI analysis daily. — [@ktest_one](https://x.com/ktest_one)
- 푸망 (주식회사 푸른망아지, founded Jul 2020): 360+ tests, 75M cumulative plays, 790K members. Formats include "푸망 시리즈" multi-part tests, the "심테공방" user-made test builder, and many brand, institution, and organization collaboration tests. Source is a wiki snapshot, last edited 2026-06-24, and is not independently verified. — [namu wiki (POOMANG)](https://namu.moe/w/POOMANG)
- 에겐/테토 test (2025): four types (테토남/에겐남/테토녀/에겐녀), likened to testosterone and estrogen but explicitly not biological. The concept was made concrete by Instagram webtoon artist 내쪼 (65K followers). The social play app 타입스 reported 714,250 participants as of May 23, 2025. Used mainly to analyze dating tendencies. Criticized for its gender binary. — [Daum (2025-05-23)](https://v.daum.net/v/20250523163004140)

This comes from search snippets; which of these two articles carries the quote was not verified. — [Nate News (2025-08-05)](https://news.nate.com/view/20250805n34882); [Daum IN서치 (2025-05-21)](https://v.daum.net/v/20250521081235762)
- Korea 2026 rotation: SBTI (consumption and behavior), HSP, and attachment-type tests. — [CIVICNEWS (2026-04-22)](https://www.civicnews.com/news/articleView.html?idxno=39708)
- Brand psych-test marketing in Korea is long established. Example: 캐릿's "2020 트렌드 능력고사" drew 1.83M participants (2020, old), and 캐릿 also produces collaboration tests for brands. — [캐릿](https://www.careet.net/210)
- Crossover evidence: LoveType16 operates a Korea version (1.5M UU) and a Taiwan version (6M UU) from a Japanese operator. That shows Japan→Korea and Japan→Taiwan flow, not only Korea→Japan. — [PR TIMES (ラブキャラ)](https://prtimes.jp/main/html/rd/p/000000025.000172366.html)

### Inferences
- The Korean sustain model is a portfolio with fast production cadence (new tests every 1–2 weeks), with a hit rate high enough that a few 10M-visitor tests carry the platform. It is not a one-hit site. B2B collaboration tests convert viral credibility into fees.
- The listed Japanese site is the opposite: a single hit with one format, built by 2 people, with no portfolio. That explains its steep decay once SNS promotion stopped.
- Its existing Korean version could be a channel, but Korea's 16-type romance space already hosts LoveType16's Korea edition (1.5M UU), plus 케이테스트 and 푸망.
- 에겐/테토-style gender-coded "vibe" axes are a possible import, but they carry reputational risk (binary and stereotype criticism) in Japan.

### Gaps
- No 2025–2026 revenue figures for any Korean test platform were found.
- No confirmation of whether 에겐/テト diagnoses trended in Japan; Japanese searches returned nothing relevant.
- 케이테스트's 2025–2026 traffic is not reported.

## Q4. Typical life cycle of a viral diagnosis site, and what keeps some alive

### Takeaway
The pattern is a spike lasting weeks, driven by UGC screenshots and the operator's own short-video SNS, followed by roughly 95% decay within 3 months once promotion stops (the listing's own data). Longevity correlates with continuous new diagnoses (portfolio), owned CRM (LINE), new formats on the same IP (64-type, media collabs, AI chat), overseas versions, and an IP and corporate layer. Single-format sites without an ongoing SNS operation decay toward a low residual base.

### Cited Findings
- Decay example: 19.45M PV in June 2026, then about 850–874K in September (about −95.5%), then about 400K expected in October (about −98%). The operator attributes the decline mainly to stopping its own SNS in July, and says the site still keeps some PV from UGC and word of mouth alone. — [ラッコM&A listing 23364](https://rakkoma.com/project/detail/23364)
- Revenue decay in the same case: ¥1.99M (Jun), ¥1.49M (Jul), ¥0.54M (Aug), ¥0.58M (Sep). — [ラッコM&A listing 23364](https://rakkoma.com/project/detail/23364)
- Spike traffic has low engagement. An indie web service's PV tripled in June 2025 (63K→194K), but revenue was flat (¥18.6K). RPM fell from about ¥280 to ¥96 because spike visitors bounced before ads loaded. Author's conclusion: "エンゲージメントのないPVは収益に貢献しない". — [Qiita](https://qiita.com/pikachu0203/items/8241585e0b3114891615)
- Sustain example, ラブキャラ: one core diagnosis, extended to a 64-type variant, a media co-development (ABEMA モテタイプ診断), a 500K LINE base, Taiwan and Korea editions, and an AI subscription-type product, with outside capital. — [PR TIMES (ラブキャラ)](https://prtimes.jp/main/html/rd/p/000000025.000172366.html); [THE BRIDGE](https://thebridge.jp/2026/06/yoani-group-invests-in-lovechara)
- Sustain example, Korean platforms: continuous production (about 100 tests in 2 years; 2 weeks per test; 5–10M monthly participants) at 케이테스트. — [Harper's BAZAAR Korea](https://www.harpersbazaar.co.kr/article/77768). 푸망: 360+ tests, series formats, and a user-generated test builder. — [namu wiki](https://namu.moe/w/POOMANG)
- Re-engagement via "retest" seasonality: 푸망's "2026년 NEW MBTI" (Jan 2026) framed as "has my MBTI changed?". — [Poomang Threads](https://www.threads.com/@poomang_official/post/DTuD4N8koyu/). In Japan, 56.2% of Gen Z have taken MBTI multiple times. — [Z-SOZOKEN](https://prtimes.jp/main/html/rd/p/000000096.000104461.html)
- Evergreen portals: classic single-axis diagnoses (サイコパス, 精神年齢) keep pulling hundreds of thousands to about a million users per half-year on a diagnosis portal. — [東京Days](https://tokyo.publishing.3rd-in.co.jp/article/047ccf6e-9dc9-11f1-a8c0-9ca3ba08e13f)

### Inferences
- Approximate shape, from one data point plus analogues: a ramp of about 1–3 months while the operator runs SNS, a peak lasting a few weeks, then about −70% in month 2, about −95% by month 3, and a residual tail of about 2–5% of peak sustained by organic UGC. This is n=1 and should not be treated as a general law.
- The listing's own claim that growth requires "short-video SNS operation" means the asset's value depends on the buyer's SNS capability. The traffic was "rented" from the operator's account, not owned.
- Features that plausibly extend life: compatibility (each sharer recruits a second person), a bulletin board or community (repeat visits), and characters that can become stickers or goods. In the listing case, these features did not prevent the −95% decay without SNS, so they are not sufficient on their own.

### Gaps
- No published second decay curve for a Japanese diagnosis site was found (e.g., LoveType16's or へびべあ's traffic history).
- No data on how compatibility or bulletin-board features affect retention.

## Q5. Post-peak metrics and M&A valuations / listing prices for diagnosis sites

### Takeaway
Public comparables are thin. The listed site at ¥12.8M (about 22x latest monthly profit, about 2.7x its cumulative 5-month revenue) is by far the largest diagnosis asset on ラッコM&A. Other diagnosis-related listings range from ¥80K to ¥2.25M, and those whose revenue has decayed trade at single- to low-double-digit multiples of current monthly profit. No diagnosis-site listings were found on TRANBI or BATONZ.

### Cited Findings
- Listed site: ¥12.8M asking; latest monthly profit ¥572,992 (shown as a 23-month multiple). — [ラッコM&A listing 23364](https://rakkoma.com/project/detail/23364)
- ラッコM&A diagnosis-search comparables as of Oct 2026:
  - 顔診断アプリ (16K DL): ¥450K, sold in 58 days. Monthly revenue now ¥30K (avg ¥75.8K, peak ¥220K). That is about 15x current monthly profit.
  - Fortune/psychology product rights with diagnosis content: ¥80K. Revenue fell from a ¥611K peak to ¥2,388.
  - キャラクター型占い・自己理解メディア with a free diagnosis: ¥300K.
  - 恋愛・星座相性メディア: ¥2.25M with about 12K PV and ¥0 revenue (closed).
  - 四柱推命 report site: ¥900K, 128 PV, ¥0.
  - Others are small tool-attached blogs at ¥80K–¥490K.
  - [ラッコM&A 診断 search](https://rakkoma.com/project/list?search_word=%E8%A8%BA%E6%96%AD)
- ラッコM&A fees: the seller pays no site-sale fee; the buyer pays 5% of the deal value (minimum ¥55,000). Business and share transfers are also supported from Oct 2026. — [ラッコ株式会社 service page](https://rakko.inc/services/ma/) (via search summary)
- TRANBI: sellers pay nothing; buyers pay a monthly plan of ¥3,980–19,800. No personality-diagnosis-site listings were surfaced. — [meetsmore (TRANBI overview)](https://meetsmore.com/products/tranbi)

### Inferences
- The ¥12.8M price is set on September run-rate profit, but October PV is already about half of September's. On a forward basis the multiple is closer to about 45–50x monthly profit, unless the buyer restarts SNS. The price effectively charges for the IP, codebase, and "proven 19M-PV playbook", not for the cash flow.
- Downside comps (the fortune product falling from a ¥611K peak to an ¥80K ask; the face app falling from ¥220K peak revenue to a ¥450K ask) show that once viral revenue decays, diagnosis assets reprice to roughly 10–20x residual monthly profit.
- For a buyer, the excluded LINE stamps and note account remove the clearest non-ad IP revenue and the content channel. The bundle being sold is mainly code, a domain, and character art. Clarifying copyright ownership of the character art and the right to make derivatives is critical.
- Build-vs-buy: the listing's own cost data (infra about ¥5K/month pre-viral; dev via an AI coding tool at about ¥16–37K/month) suggests a comparable site can be built for well under ¥1M. The scarce input is SNS distribution skill and a hit concept, not code.

### Gaps
- No completed-deal prices for viral diagnosis sites (as opposed to asking prices) were found.
- No BATONZ, M&Aクラウド, or Flippa Japanese diagnosis-site comparables were found.
- No public RPM benchmarks for Japanese teen-female entertainment traffic were found, beyond the n=1 derived figures above.

## Q6. Is the space saturated, and where are the white spaces?

### Takeaway
The "16-type × original character × romance × compatibility" lane is crowded: LoveType16 dominates, with multiple 2026 clones (bears, self-esteem, 推し活, AI pair reports). New entrants in that exact lane face a dominant incumbent plus low copy cost. White spaces suggested by the evidence are axes beyond MBTI, other-perspective and visual typing, purpose-built B2B/brand collaboration diagnoses, and monetizing the "type-linked product" interest that over half of Gen Z report. Context-specific angles (新学期/クラス替え, family, workplace) lack sourced demand data and remain hypotheses.

### Cited Findings
- Crowding signals: May–June 2026 alone saw multiple 16-type character diagnoses, including へびべあ ([coki](https://coki.jp/article/column/83171/)), 自己肯定感診断 ([Trepo](https://trepo.jp/life-style/338957/)), and 推し活タイプ診断 ([PR TIMES](https://prtimes.jp/main/html/rd/p/000000036.000135510.html)). The incumbent reports 100M diagnoses and 50M UU ([PR TIMES (ラブキャラ)](https://prtimes.jp/main/html/rd/p/000000025.000172366.html)).
- Demand for type-linked products: 52.2% of Gen Z are interested, rising to 82.4% among active searchers. — [Z-SOZOKEN](https://prtimes.jp/main/html/rd/p/000000096.000104461.html)
- Compatibility content is widely viewed (43.4%), but Gen Z keeps it at arm's length ("相性は関係の答えにしない"). — [Z-SOZOKEN ch.7](https://prtimes.jp/main/html/rd/p/000000103.000104461.html)
- Usage of the pair-compatibility product skews one-sided: 85.4% look up only one person, and 76.3% of self-entered types are F types. — [PR TIMES (M16)](https://prtimes.jp/main/html/rd/p/000000007.000175516.html)
- "How others see me" and visual typing are rising: キャラクターコード (face + survey) ([PERFECT](https://www.perfectcorp.com/ja/consumer/blog/makeup/character-code-diagnosis-ja)) and 顔のMBTI on TikTok/X, Oct 2026 ([PERFECT](https://www.perfectcorp.com/ja/consumer/blog/ai-skincare/16-types-face-analysis)).
- Korea's newer axes: 에겐/테토 (gender-coded vibe; [Daum](https://v.daum.net/v/20250523163004140)), SBTI (consumption), HSP (sensitivity), and attachment style ([CIVICNEWS](https://www.civicnews.com/news/articleView.html?idxno=39708)).
- Backlash risk: the Korean binary/stereotype critique of 에겐/테토 ([Daum](https://v.daum.net/v/20250523163004140)); Z-SOZOKEN warns about stereotyping by 4-letter types ([Z-SOZOKEN](https://prtimes.jp/main/html/rd/p/000000096.000104461.html)); Forbes Japan on Gen Z disliking MBTI "決めつけ" ([Forbes Japan](https://forbesjapan.com/articles/detail/104325)).
- Evergreen non-MBTI single-axis diagnoses (サイコパス, 精神年齢, モテ度) still draw large audiences. — [東京Days](https://tokyo.publishing.3rd-in.co.jp/article/047ccf6e-9dc9-11f1-a8c0-9ca3ba08e13f)
- Brands commission diagnosis content for campaigns: JR東海 (event attendee data), game launches via official LINE, anime IP. — [zaikei](https://zaikei.co.jp/releases/3306423/); [premium.gamepedia](https://premium.gamepedia.jp/hobby/archives/137780)

### Inferences
- Underserved angles (hypotheses grounded in the above):
  - (a) Post-MBTI axes not yet popularized in Japan: attachment style, HSP/sensitivity, consumption type (SBTI-like), or a toned-down Japanese take on gender-neutral 雰囲気 vibes. Korea already validated each.
  - (b) Other-perspective or friend-rated diagnoses (friends answer about you). These pull in a second person more naturally than self-entered compatibility, and match the "他人から見た" trend.
  - (c) Group or class formats for 新学期/クラス替え: a whole friend group gets a "team composition" card. This extends one-to-one compatibility to many-to-many sharing.
  - (d) 推し×type crossovers using original characters rather than licensed IP.
  - (e) Monetization white space: type-linked goods, stickers, and wallpaper. More than half of Gen Z report interest, yet the listing's operator excluded its LINE stamps from sale, and ラブキャラ lists goods only as a plan.
  - (f) A B2B line: collaboration diagnoses for brands and events, which Korean operators and 캐릿 monetize.
- A tone white space may exist in "heavy, self-deprecating, あるある" humor (へびべあ's "重め" love bears suggests resonance). This rests on one case only.
- Entering head-on with another 16-type romance-character site carries the highest saturation risk. Differentiation must come from a new axis or a new social mechanic, not new art alone.

### Gaps
- No sourced data on demand by context (新学期, クラス替え, 職場, 家族); no seasonality data for diagnosis search or traffic in Japan.
- No data on how many 16-type character diagnoses launched in Japan in 2025–2026 overall, so saturation is judged qualitatively.
- No evidence on willingness to pay for paid detailed reports in Japan (ラブチャッピー's pricing is undisclosed).
